This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your personal information when you use the ZenBoard Chrome Extension. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is handled securely. By using ZenBoard, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. 1. Information We Collect To provide you with the best experience, we collect the following types of information: A. Information You Provide Directly Account Information: When you register for an account using your email/password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address and basic profile information. User Content: Bookmarks, links, board names, page structures, custom wallpapers, and preferences (such as themes and layouts) that you create and save within ZenBoard. Support Inquiries: Information you provide when you contact us for help or support (e.g., your email address and message contents). B. Automatically Collected Information Storage & Sync Data: Your boards, bookmarks, and settings are stored locally in your browser via Chrome Storage and, when enabled, synced securely across your devices. Subscription Status: Basic subscription status (such as active trial, active Pro plan, or canceled status) used solely to enable ZenBoard Pro features. 2. All payment transactions are handled directly by Polar in accordance with their security standards. For more details, please review Polar's Privacy Policy at: https://polar.sh/legal/privacy 3. How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect solely to: - Provide, maintain, and personalize your ZenBoard dashboard and bookmark manager. - Sync your bookmarks, boards, and custom preferences across your devices. - Verify and manage your ZenBoard Pro subscription and 7-day free trial. - Respond to your customer support requests and improve the extension. 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, bookmarks, or browsing data to third parties. We do not track your general browsing history outside of the ZenBoard extension. We only share necessary information with trusted service providers (such as Polar for payment processing and cloud infrastructure providers for sync) strictly to operate the service. 5. Browser Permissions ZenBoard only requests the Chrome permissions strictly necessary to function, such as managing bookmarks, storing your preferences, and displaying your custom new-tab dashboard. 6. Data Security and Retention We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your local bookmarks and preferences remain on your device until you clear your extension data or uninstall ZenBoard.
