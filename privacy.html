Privacy Policy for ZenBoard Effective Date: November 12, 2026 Thank you for choosing ZenBoard ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your personal information when you use the ZenBoard Chrome Extension (the "Extension"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data is handled securely. By using ZenBoard, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. 1. Information We Collect To provide you with the best experience, we collect the following types of information: A. Information You Provide Directly Account Information: When you register for an account using your email/password or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address, basic profile information (like your name or profile picture, if provided by Google), and a unique User ID. User Content: Bookmarks, links, board names, page structures, custom wallpapers, and preferences (such as themes and layouts) that you create and save within the Extension. Support Inquiries: Information you provide when you contact us for help or support (e.g., your email address and message contents). B. Automatically Collected Information Local Storage Data: The Extension stores your user content locally in your browser to provide fast, seamless, and offline access to your bookmarks. 2. How We Use Your Information We use the collected information strictly to provide and improve the Extension's core functionality. Specifically, we use your data to: Enable Core Features: Allow you to save, organize, and manage your bookmarks and boards. Cloud Synchronization: Sync your bookmarks and settings across multiple devices using Google Firebase Firestore (only if you are logged in). Authentication: Verify your identity and manage your trial or premium subscription status securely. Customer Support: Respond to your questions, bug reports, and feature requests. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Disclosure: The ZenBoard extension's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell your personal data to any third parties. 3. Third-Party Services We Use We utilize trusted third-party services to operate ZenBoard securely. These services have their own privacy policies: Google Firebase & Firestore: Used for user authentication (Email/Google Sign-In) and secure cloud database synchronization. All data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit and at rest. Firebase Privacy Policy Lemon Squeezy: Used as our Merchant of Record to securely process subscription payments. ZenBoard does not process or store your credit card details on our servers. Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy 4. Data Storage and Security Security Measures: We use industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption, to protect your data during transmission between your browser and our cloud servers (Google Firebase). Data Retention: We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Trash Management: Items you delete within the Extension are moved to a "Trash" folder and are permanently and automatically deleted after 30 days. 5. Your Data Rights and Controls You have full control over your data: Access and Edit: You can view and edit all your bookmarks, boards, and settings directly within the Extension. Delete Data: You can permanently delete any links, boards, or custom wallpapers at any time. Account Deletion: You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated cloud data by contacting our support email. 6. Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. 7.
